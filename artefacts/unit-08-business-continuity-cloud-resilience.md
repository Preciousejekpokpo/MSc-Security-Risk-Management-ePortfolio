# Unit 8 | Business Continuity and Cloud Resilience

## Learning Activities

The Unit 8 lecturecasts covered business continuity and disaster recovery, disaster recovery during digitalisation, single versus multiple suppliers, hybrid cloud, cloud-native applications and the shared responsibility model.

## Application to Risk Management

These topics helped connect recovery planning with wider operational dependencies. Supplier diversification may reduce reliance on one provider, but additional suppliers also introduce coordination and quality considerations. Similarly, cloud infrastructure may support resilience without guaranteeing that business services can recover within the required RTO and RPO.

These considerations informed my Unit 11 Pampered Pets assessment, particularly the proposed multi-region disaster recovery architecture. The subsequent feedback identified gaps in its failover configuration, capacity and cost justification, reinforcing the importance of testing recovery arrangements against operational requirements rather than relying on architectural assumptions.
