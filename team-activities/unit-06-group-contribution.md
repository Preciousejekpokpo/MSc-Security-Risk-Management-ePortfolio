# Unit 6 | Group Risk Assessment and Individual Contribution

Although all four members contributed research, my main responsibility was integrating and critically reviewing the report. Differences in terminology and risk assessment approaches required reconciliation, while working across time zones meant that discussions took place largely through WhatsApp. This allowed us to maintain progress but restricted opportunities to examine assumptions collectively.

The 74% feedback recognised our scrutiny of projected business benefits while questioning the justification of risk ratings and residual-risk treatment. Looking back, I had concentrated on producing a coherent assessment without examining whether the underlying judgements were equally defensible. This distinction exposed a weakness in how I approached integration, since consistency in presentation could conceal differences in the evidence supporting individual contributions.
