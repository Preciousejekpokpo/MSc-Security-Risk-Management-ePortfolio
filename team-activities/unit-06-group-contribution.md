# Unit 6 | Group Risk Assessment and Individual Contribution

Although the Unit 6 report drew on research from all four members, I took responsibility for bringing the contributions together, reviewing the analysis and developing a consistent argument. This required reconciling differences in terminology and approaches to risk assessment, while communication across time zones meant that much of our discussion took place asynchronously. WhatsApp made collaboration manageable, although it also limited opportunities to examine assumptions collectively before the report was finalised.

The lecturer's feedback recognised our critical treatment of the projected growth and sourcing figures, but questioned the justification of individual risk scores and the limited assessment of residual exposure. Looking back, I had concentrated on ensuring the arguments were coherent without scrutinising the underlying ratings to the same standard. This exposed a weakness in how I approached the integration process, where bringing different contributions into agreement needed to be accompanied by more systematic examination of the evidence supporting them.
