# Project Evaluation | From Unit 6 to Unit 11

The Unit 6 assessment treated digitalisation as a decision requiring justification rather than an inevitable improvement. Our phased recommendation challenged optimistic business projections, but the 74% feedback exposed poorly justified risk scores and insufficient evidence that proposed controls would reduce exposure.

Unit 11 made these judgements more transparent through fault trees and Monte Carlo simulation, connecting initiating events and failed safeguards to product quality and availability. Sensitivity analysis identified influential assumptions, yet the 73% feedback challenged their evidential basis. Without operational data for control performance or modelling shared failures, numerical precision risked overstating confidence.

The disaster recovery proposal reflected a similar limitation. Its attention to end-to-end checkout was valuable, but unspecified failover timings, capacity and costs prevented assurance that the architecture would meet its targets. Across both submissions, the key development needed was to test the evidence supporting recommendations, not simply make the models more elaborate.
